# Vista medicine guide, batch m1: research log

Companion to `m1.json` (14 entries: 10 new, 4 existing entries with a `dose` block added). Built 15 November 2026. For each entry this log lists every claim and dose number and the exact source and section it came from. Generated by `kb2/scripts/build_m1.py`, which also checks ids, condition ids, check keys, units, ASCII-only text and that the four existing entries are copied unchanged from `kb/kb_meds.json` apart from the new `dose` key.

## Ground rules applied

- Doses come only from official labelling: FDA Drug Facts / prescribing information on DailyMed (downloaded as SPL XML through the DailyMed v2 API and converted to text, saved in `kb2/dl/*.xml` and `*.txt`) and UK SmPCs on emc (saved in `kb2/dl/emc*.html` / `.txt`). Dose wording in `label` and `max` follows the label; figures in brackets (for example "(325 mg each)", "(3,900 mg)") are tablet count multiplied by the strength printed in the same label's "Active ingredient" line.
- Spaceflight notes come from NASA documents already verified for `kb_meds` (local copies in `kb/dl_emp`, `kb/dl_ochmo`, `kb/dl_ntrs2`, `kb/src`, `kb/dl_epmc`, re-read for this batch) plus one new NTRS record (Fortney 1994, opened via the NTRS API).
- `calc` is true for over-the-counter style medicines a crew member could take under a standing order; false for ondansetron (prescription only; QT-prolongation warning).
- `mgPerKg` is null everywhere: no label gives an adult per-kg dose in mg. The Dioralyte SmPC gives a daily fluid volume of 20 to 40 mL/kg; it is in the `label` text only (see "Not sourced / not encoded").

### Gate thresholds

Gates fire only where the label's warnings name the condition. The label gives the condition; the number comes from Vista's own sourced thresholds in `docs/evidence/clinical-thresholds.md`:

| Gate | Label basis | Number and its source |
|---|---|---|
| bp sys >= 140, bp dia >= 90 (aspirin, naproxen, ibuprofen) | Drug Facts "Ask a doctor before use if ... you have high blood pressure" (Bayer Genuine Aspirin; Aleve Tablets - Blueshield; Advil tablets) | 140/90 = stage 2 hypertension, 2025 AHA/ACC guideline (clinical-thresholds.md CLN-01 and the blood-pressure table row) |
| temp >= 38.0 (loperamide) | Imodium A-D Drug Facts "Ask a doctor before use if you have fever" | 38.0 deg C = CDC fever definition (clinical-thresholds.md CLN-11) |

No gate was added for the US diclofenac (Voltaren) high-blood-pressure warning, because the entry uses the UK Voltarol SmPC, which does not carry it. No label in this batch ties a warning to low fluid intake, slow reaction time, SpO2 or glucose, so no such gates were added.

## Not sourced / not encoded

- No adult mg-per-kg dose exists in any label used, so `mgPerKg` is null for all 14 entries. Dioralyte's 20 to 40 mL/kg/day is in the `label` text only, because it is a daily fluid volume, not an mg-per-kg dose.
- Ondansetron: the US label has no motion-sickness indication or dose and no general oral 24-hour maximum; `maxSingle` and `maxDay` are null. The ISS kit's 8 mg strength matches the label's radiotherapy dose, but no in-flight dosing schedule was found in an opened source.
- Diclofenac gel: the label gives grams per application, not a 24-hour gram maximum; encoded as up to 4 applications a day.
- Aspirin's 3-tablets-every-6-hours option cannot be expressed in a single `everyH`; `everyH` is 4 (for 1 to 2 tablets) and the 6-hour rule is in the `label` text.
- No spaceflight-specific data was found for docusate, calcium carbonate antacids or naproxen use in flight; their `space` notes are limited to kit-list status, the SLEP shelf-life figure and NASA's nutrient-absorption warning.
- Simethicone has no condition link: none of the condition ids covers gas or bloating.
- No label in the batch supports gates on fluid intake, reaction time, sleep, SpO2 or glucose, so none were added.
