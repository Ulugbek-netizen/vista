# Void Voyagers medicine guide, batch m3: research log

Companion to `m3.json` (13 entries: 10 new, 3 existing entries with a `dose` block added). Built 15 November 2026. `m3.json` is generated by `m3_build.py` in the same folder, which also checks the JSON shape, condition ids, check keys, units and ASCII.

## Ground rules applied

- Dose numbers come only from US FDA labels on DailyMed (Structured Product Labels downloaded through the DailyMed v2 API as XML, converted to text and searched; local copies in `kb2/dl/` and `kb2/dl/m3/`). Numbers are copied as the label states them. Spaceflight notes come from NASA documents and peer-reviewed papers already verified for `kb_meds.json` (local copies in `kb/`, re-read for this batch).
- `calc` is `false` for every entry in this batch. All 13 are prescription medicines: antibiotics and an antiviral need a diagnosis; glyceryl trinitrate and metoprolol are heart and blood-pressure drugs (excluded by the task rules); tamsulosin, potassium citrate and alendronate are given only on the flight surgeon's decision (the existing entries say so).
- `maxDay` is filled only where the label states a 24-hour (or per-day) cap. Where the label gives regimens but no cap, `maxDay` is `null` and the `max` text says so. `maxSingle` is the highest single adult dose in the label regimens for the conditions the entry is linked to (exceptions noted per entry). `everyH` is filled only where the label states "every N hours" or "once daily" (24) or "once weekly" (168).
- Gates were added only where the label gives a number that matches a Void Voyagers check. Only one qualifies (metoprolol, systolic < 100). See "Gates considered and not added".
- ISS kit list: NASA ISS Emergency Medical Procedures Manual, section 3.001 Medical Kit Contents and Reference (pages dated 9 Apr 2015), FOIA release 2016, local copy `kb/dl_emp/emp2016.txt`. Page numbers below are the section's printed "Page N of 27". The text layout of the tables is scrambled in the PDF text, so only the drug names and the section headings are used, not the strength columns.
- Reichard 2023 page numbers are the printed page numbers (checked with pdftotext per page: PDF page 32 = printed 26, PDF page 44 = printed 38).

## Gates considered and not added

- Glyceryl trinitrate: the labels warn about patients "already hypotensive" and about shock, but give no blood-pressure number. No numeric gate.
- Tamsulosin: orthostasis warning, no number.
- Ciprofloxacin, valaciclovir, doxycycline: "adequate hydration" / "fluids liberally", no fluid volume, so no `water` gate.
- Potassium citrate: contraindicated in "acute dehydration" and needs urine at least 2 litres a day; urine volume is not Void Voyagers's `water` (intake) check, so no gate.
- Metoprolol heart rate: TOPROL-XL and LOPRESSOR contraindicate "severe bradycardia" without a number. LOPRESSOR's adverse-reaction table defines bradycardia as heart rate < 40 in one trial; that is a trial definition, not a contraindication, so no `hr` gate.
- Azithromycin: QT and bradyarrhythmia risks, no number.

## Not sourced or left out

- No spaceflight use record for nitrofurantoin (stated in the entry).
